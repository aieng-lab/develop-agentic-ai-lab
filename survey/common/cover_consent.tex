\blockheading{Why we are asking}

\noindent
The goal of this lab is that you understand how agentic systems work, not merely that your team ships a
working harness. To find out whether the lab actually achieves that, we measure what you know about
agentic systems \emph{before} the lab starts and \emph{again after} it ends. Comparing the two tells us
where the lab teaches well and where we need to improve it. The same questionnaire structure is used at
both points in time.

\blockheading{This is voluntary and does not affect your grade}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=0.25em,topsep=0.3em]
  \item Participation is entirely \textbf{voluntary}. You may skip any question, or stop at any point.
  \item Your answers have \textbf{no influence whatsoever on your grade}. They are not part of the daily
        reports, the presentations, the written report, or the oral exam.
  \item The questionnaire is \textbf{pseudonymous} such that only you know the pseudonym: do not write your name, your FIM account, or your matriculation
        number anywhere on this form.
  \item The knowledge questions are \textbf{not an exam}. In the pre-test especially, most topics have not been
        taught yet, so a low score is entirely expected.
        Please do not guess wildly --- use the ``I do not know'' box instead. That is more useful
        to us than a lucky guess.
  \item The full answer key will be published after the post-test, so you can learn from them.
\end{itemize}

\blockheading{Data protection}

\noindent
We process only the answers on this form, linked by the self-generated code below --- no names and no
identifiers that let us contact or identify you. Data are stored on university infrastructure, evaluated
in aggregate, and reported only in aggregate. No result will be reported
in a way that could single out an individual (groups smaller than five are not reported separately).
Legal basis is your consent, which you may withdraw --- though once forms are pooled we can no longer
locate an individual questionnaire, so withdrawal is only possible before pooling. Questions about the
survey or the data: \texttt{steffen.herbold@uni-passau.de}.
